LUMEA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026 · Policy version 2.1 · App version 1.1.0

Summary

  • All cocktail recipes and images are bundled inside the app; no internet connection is needed to read them.
  • The free version shows ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which processes your advertising identifier and an approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • If you buy "Remove ads", the advertising software is never initialised — not a single ad request is sent.
  • No account is required. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • There is no analytics and no crash-reporting software in the app.
  • At launch the app asks whether you are over 18. The answer stays on your device; no date of birth or ID is requested. If you answer under 18, the app shows alcohol-free recipes only.

2. Scope

3. Data we process

3.1 Information that stays on your device

The following is stored in your device's local storage and is never sent to us or to any server:

  • Your language preference (Turkish / English)
  • Your age declaration — the answer you give to the "Are you over 18?" question at launch. Only 18+ or under 18 is stored; we never ask for or keep a date of birth, an exact age or any identity document. This declaration decides which recipes are shown: if you are under 18, the app lists alcohol-free recipes only.
  • Your "What's in my bar?" list — the ingredients you tick
  • An ad-free flag — a simple marker so the app stays ad-free even when offline

Uninstalling the app deletes this information.

3.2 Advertising (free version only)

Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). When an ad is shown, Google processes:

CategoryExampleWhy
Advertising identifier Android Advertising ID (AAID); on iOS the IDFA, only if you allow it Ad selection, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement
Device information Model, OS version, screen size, language, carrier, network type Serving a correctly sized and formatted ad
Approximate location Country/city level, derived from your IP address Regional ad eligibility and legal requirements
Diagnostic information Performance of the app and the ad SDK: launch time, hang rate, energy usage Stability and performance measurement of the ad SDK
Ad interaction Ad request, impression, click Reporting and publisher payment

No precise (GPS) location is used. The app does not request location permission. For how Google uses this data, see Google — Business Data Responsibility and How Google uses information from apps that use our services.

3.3 Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. Your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.) are processed directly by Google; we never see or store them. The app only receives a yes/no answer from Google Play as to whether the product has been purchased. The purchase is tied to your Google account, which is why "Restore purchases" works on a new device.

4. Data we do not collect

LUMEA does not request, collect or process:

  • Name, email address, phone number, date of birth
  • Precise (GPS) location
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone, camera
  • Special category data such as health, biometric, religious or political data
  • Any account or login information — the app has no account system

The app contains no separate analytics SDK (no Firebase Analytics, no Google Analytics) and no crash-reporting SDK (no Crashlytics). Which recipes you read, which ingredients you select and how long you use the app are never transmitted to us. Only the advertising SDK processes the measurement and diagnostic data listed in section 3.2 above, for its own ad reporting; we have no access to it.

6. Consent and ad personalisation

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent screen is shown the first time you open the app, using Google's User Messaging Platform. There you can accept or refuse personalised advertising.

  • If you accept: ads may be selected based on your interests.
  • If you refuse: ads are still shown, but they are not personalised and no profiling takes place.

You can change your decision at any time: tap "Privacy options" in the "LUMEA without ads" section near the bottom of the app. This button is only visible to users in the regions where it applies.

7. Removing ads

If you purchase the "Remove ads" product from inside the app:

  • The advertising software (Google Mobile Ads SDK) is never initialised;
  • Your advertising identifier is not read;
  • No ad-related network request is made at all.

This is not merely hiding the banner in the interface — the entire advertising stack is disabled. The purchase is one-time and applies to every device signed in with the same Google account.

9. Retention

  • On-device preferences: until you delete them or uninstall the app.

We operate no servers and no databases, so we hold no personal data about you.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR (Arts. 15–22) and the KVKK (Art. 11) you may:

  • Ask whether we process your personal data and obtain access to it
  • Request rectification, erasure or destruction
  • Object to processing and request restriction
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Object to automated decisions that affect you
  • Claim compensation for damage suffered

Send requests to [email protected]; we respond within 30 days. Because we hold no records about you, access and erasure requests usually have to be directed to Google — we will point you to the right place. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority (GDPR) or with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK).

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell your personal information. However, personalised advertising may qualify as "sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising" under the CPRA. To opt out, use the "Privacy options" screen in the app, turn off ad personalisation in your device settings, or purchase "Remove ads".

11. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID

  • Android: Settings → Google → All services → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. Once deleted, apps can no longer access the identifier for personalisation.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If Allow Apps to Request to Track is off, the IDFA is not shared. When the iOS version of LUMEA asks for tracking permission you may choose "Ask App Not to Track"; the app continues to work with all features.

13. Children and age rating

LUMEA contains alcoholic drink recipes and is not suitable for anyone under 18. The app is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from children. The ad SDK is configured with child-directed treatment disabled and an age rating of 18+. If we learn that we have processed data belonging to someone under 18 we delete it immediately; you can report such a case to [email protected].

Please drink responsibly. Alcohol can be harmful to your health.

14. Security

The app does not connect to any server operated by us, so there is no user database on our side that could be breached. Advertising and billing traffic is encrypted with TLS. The app package is signed with a release key and uses Google Play App Signing.

15. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the "Last updated" date above. For significant changes we also notify you inside the app. If a change affects the basis of your consent — for example an expansion of advertising — the consent screen is shown again.
